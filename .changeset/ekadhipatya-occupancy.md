Apply planet occupancy in Ekadhipatya reduction. Each pair of signs one planet rules is now reduced by whether the seven planets occupy them: no reduction when both are occupied, the empty sign loses the occupied sign's score when only one is, and the larger score keeps the difference when neither is. Reduced Bhinnashtakavarga and Shodhya Pinda change for charts where a pair has an occupied sign.
