# Share Chara Karaka roles on exact ties

Chara Karakas compare the Degrees derived exactly from the stored D1 longitudes of the seven classical planets, without rounding or tolerance; the comparison uses their canonical decimal positions so binary remainder artifacts do not split the same position across Signs. When several planets occupy the same position within their Signs, their tied ranks occupy the same number of consecutive roles and every tied planet holds every occupied role; the calculation does not reject the chart, impose a secondary ranking, or insert Rahu as a fallback. Results remain role-first with seven fixed roles, each containing a canonically ordered non-empty collection of planet-and-Degree evidence, and every planet holding Atmakaraka contributes its D9 Sign to Karakamsha in that same order.
